Ketamine in Emergency and Critical Care Medicine: Indication-Based Decision Algorithm

Dr. Özgür Karcıoğlu & Dr. Satuk Buğra Han Bozatlı — an interactive companion to "Ketamine in Emergency and Critical Care Medicine: A Narrative Review of Sedation, Analgesia, and Resuscitative Interventions."

Academic / educational reference tool, not a substitute for institutional protocol or bedside clinical judgement. Verify any dose or timing against current local protocol before clinical use. Reference verification for this tool differs from the rest of this atlas — see the Status section of README.md and the References list below.

Scroll sideways to see all five lanes — the diagram keeps its full desktop layout on mobile.

Select any box in the diagram — the trigger, the indication, a dose, a decision, or one of the reference panels — to read what it means, why the algorithm calls for it, and the sources behind it.
